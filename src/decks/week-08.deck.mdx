# Recommendation Algorithms

Does picking what you already like change what you're offered next?

---

## A familiar scene

You watch one workplace comedy on a slow evening. Within a few sessions your
home screen is visibly heavier on comedies — not because you told it your
preferences, and not because you watched anything else in between.

---

## This week's question

A recommendation feed never has to say no to you. Do five small,
individually reasonable picks in a row change what you're even shown by the
sixth?

## The interactive experiment: The Narrowing Feed

Five rounds. Each shows four categories; pick one. The next round's mix
updates based only on your pick history, following a fixed, simplified rule
— not a real streaming algorithm, just enough of the same shape.

## Takeaway

Nothing here adapted to guess your "real" taste. A handful of individually
small picks compounded into a visibly smaller menu — with no single step
where anyone decided to remove an option.
