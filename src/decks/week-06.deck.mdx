# Time Perception

Why can three episodes feel shorter than fifteen minutes of waiting?

---

## A familiar scene

You start an episode at 9pm meaning to watch one. Three episodes and ninety
gripping minutes later, the clock reads almost midnight — and the honest
reaction is closer to confusion than guilt.

---

## This week's question

Nobody has a stopwatch running in their head. A rough estimate of "how long
was that" gets built from something other than the clock. What, exactly?

## The interactive experiment: The Guessing Clock

Three rounds, each running for the exact same real length of time. No
visible clock. When a round ends, guess how many seconds just passed.

- **Round 1** — baseline, plain and unchanging
- **Round 2** — absorbing, something new every second
- **Round 3** — monotonous, the same line repeating on a slow beat

---

{/* _class: banner */}

## Takeaway

Three identical durations, three different guesses. Scale that up, and a
gripping binge is the "absorbing" round writ large.
