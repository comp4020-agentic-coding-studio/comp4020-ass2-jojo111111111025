# Rewards & Reinforcement

Why does an unpredictable reward pull harder than a reliable one?

---

## A familiar scene

Checking the mail, which arrives on roughly the same schedule, versus
refreshing a feed, which sometimes has something new after ten seconds and
sometimes after twenty minutes.

Nobody obsessively re-checks the mailbox. Plenty of people re-check a feed
dozens of times an hour.

---

## This week's question

Two people each check six times and each get rewarded twice. Identical
quantity — but ask how compelling it felt to keep checking, and the answers
can be completely different. **When** the reward lands is what mattered.

## The interactive experiment: Two Checking Habits

Two short rounds of the same task — press a button to check for a new
episode. Both rounds hand out the exact same total number of rewards. Watch
for *when* they land, not how many there are.

---

{/* _class: banner */}

## Takeaway

The number of rewards was identical in both rounds. If the variable round
felt harder to stop, that's timing — not quantity — doing the pulling.
