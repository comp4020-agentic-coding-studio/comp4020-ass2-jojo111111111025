# The Final Episode

What happens when the mechanisms we've studied work together?

---

## A familiar scene

Nobody sits down and consciously invokes six psychological mechanisms in
order. It just happens: an ending doesn't resolve, the next thing is
already counting down, most episodes are fine but one is unexpectedly
great, the next row of tiles looks tailored, a friend's name is attached to
one of them — and a wake-up time quietly stops being part of the decision.

---

## This week's question

Every week so far isolated one mechanism at a time, on purpose. A real
viewing session never runs one mechanism in isolation. What happens once
the mechanisms we've studied **stop taking turns and start running
together**?

## Takeaway

Across twelve weeks, one question asked from a dozen angles: why does "one
more" so rarely feel like the decision it actually is? Every mechanism
shared the same shape — not a flaw in anyone's character, but a property of
an interface built to make continuing easier than stopping, one small
design choice at a time.
