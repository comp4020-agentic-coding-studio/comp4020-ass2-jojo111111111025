# Autoplay

What changes when "continue" is the default and "stop" is the thing you have to do?

---

## A familiar scene

<FlowRow steps={["Countdown appears", "Stop button is right there", "Nothing is clicked", "Next episode starts itself"]} />

No decision ever consciously got made — because not deciding was also an option.

---

## This week's question

Every earlier mechanism made *continuing* attractive. Autoplay doesn't need
to. It only needs to make *stopping* into a task, and let default behaviour
do the rest.

---

## The interactive experiment: The Ten-Second Window

Three rounds, same countdown length every time:

- **Round 1** — baseline, stop control visible, countdown announced
- **Round 2** — control buried for a couple of seconds
- **Round 3** — control reachable, but nothing announces the countdown

---

{/* _class: banner */}

## Takeaway

The countdown never changed across rounds. What changed was how much it cost
to reach the option that was, technically, always available.
