## This week's question

Week 1 started after the choice to watch was already made. This week goes
one step earlier: **why do we press play in the first place** — before we
know almost anything real about the show?

## The interactive experiment

You'll pick one of three shows, three times. Same shows, same descriptions —
except how they're presented changes each round.

- **Round 1** — baseline, no cues
- **Round 2** — one show gets a social-proof badge
- **Round 3** — one show's description is replaced with an unresolved detail

## Takeaway

"Why do we press play" rarely has "because of the show" as its real answer.
Watch what moves between your three picks — the shows never do.
