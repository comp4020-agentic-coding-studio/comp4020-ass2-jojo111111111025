# Social Watching

What is a social cue actually doing, when the two options are otherwise the same?

---

## A familiar scene

Two unfamiliar shows, near-identical descriptions. One carries a small
label: "Trending now." You click that one — and if asked why, the honest
answer is "it seemed more popular," even though "popular" told you nothing
about whether you'd enjoy it.

---

## This week's question

Week 2 showed a "popular" badge can shift a pick between *different* shows —
so maybe it was just nudging you toward the better option. This week closes
that gap: does a social cue still move a choice between two **identical**
shows?

## The interactive experiment: Two Identical Rows

Four rounds, always the exact same two shows in the same two slots. Some
rounds carry no cue; some carry a crowd cue or a named-friend cue — the
cued slot alternates, so this isn't measuring which side you tend to click.

---

{/* _class: banner */}

## Takeaway

The shows never changed. Whatever moved your pick had to be the label
sitting next to an otherwise identical option.
