# Cliffhangers

Why does an unanswered question pull harder than an unfinished story?

---

## A familiar scene

A door opens. A line hints at who's behind it. The screen cuts to credits
before you find out.

You know the answer is one click away and isn't going anywhere — that
knowledge doesn't loosen the pull at all.

---

## This week's question

Week 1 named "unfinished information" as one reason stopping is hard. This
week asks: unfinished *how*? Not every open ending pulls the same amount.

## The interactive experiment: Rank the Cut

Four versions of the same scene — someone answering a door — written to
withhold different amounts of information. Rank them from least to most
likely to pull you into the next episode.

No hidden timer, no "correct" order — go with your own reaction.

## Takeaway

The pull isn't about how much story is left. It's about how explicitly a
question gets asked, then denied an answer.
