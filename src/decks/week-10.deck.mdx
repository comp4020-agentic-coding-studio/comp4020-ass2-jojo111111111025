# Sleep vs Entertainment

Does seeing the actual cost of "one more" change whether you pay it?

---

## A familiar scene

It's 11pm, an alarm is set for 7am, and one more 42-minute episode is
tempting. If nothing on screen ever mentions the time, "one more" doesn't
feel like a decision about sleep at all — it feels like a decision about
whether the show is good enough.

---

## This week's question

Week 7 showed what happens when a running total stays hidden. This week asks
the mirror question: what happens when a cost is shown clearly, in a unit
you already understand?

---

## The interactive experiment: The Sleep Ledger

A fixed sleep budget, a fixed wake-up time, and a running number that's
visible the whole time — not revealed only at the end. Every episode costs
the same fixed amount. Watch as many as you like, or stop to bank what's
left.

## Takeaway

Seeing a cost clearly is one thing. Whether it changes what you actually do
about it, in the moment, is a separate and genuinely open question — this
demo can only show you which pattern your own decision followed once the
excuse of "not knowing" was taken off the table.
