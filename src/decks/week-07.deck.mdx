# Binge Watching

What happens to "one more episode" once nobody is keeping count?

---

## A familiar scene

You sit down meaning to watch "an episode or two." Four hours later you
surface, and the honest answer to "how many did I watch" takes a moment to
work out — not because the number is secret, but because nothing ever asked
you to hold it in mind.

---

## This week's question

A binge is the same "one more" decision, made dozens of times in an evening.
Why does nobody — including you — ever seem to be adding it up while it's
happening?

---

## The interactive experiment: How Many Did You Actually Watch?

A small, made-up show. Watch as many episodes as you like, one at a time.
The running count stays hidden while you decide — only a chip appears per
episode. The real total is revealed only when you stop.

## Takeaway

The count was tracked the whole time — it just was never shown. A hidden
running total doesn't slow anyone down, because there was never a number to
react to.
