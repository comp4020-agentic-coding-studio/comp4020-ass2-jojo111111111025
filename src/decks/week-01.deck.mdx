# The One More Episode Effect

Week 1 — why do we keep watching when we already know we should stop?

---

## Not a willpower story

"I got sucked in" describes losing a fight.

This week is about why the fight was never fair to begin with.

---

## Two ingredients

- **Friction** — make stopping cost more effort than continuing
- **Anticipation** — leave something specific unresolved

Neither one has to change your opinion of the show.

---

{/* _class: quote */}

> Change what counts as the easy choice, and you change what people choose —
> without changing what they actually want.

---

## What the page just did to you

Three rounds, one choice, three interfaces:

1. baseline — both options equally easy
2. friction — "stop" delayed three seconds
3. anticipation — "continue" comes with an unresolved detail

---

## One participant is not a study

A single run, on one evening, is evidence the mechanism is real.

It is not evidence about your willpower.
